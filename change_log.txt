### Version 0.4 (Patch)
* **Fixes:** * Resolved the persistent **step height issue** preventing players from smoothly walking/jumping between slabs and path blocks.
* Cleaned up and removed legacy attribute files that were causing optimization issues.

### Version 0.3.1 (Patch)
* **Optimization:** Resolved attribute-related lag issues and purged unnecessary files.
* **Fixes:** Fixed metadata loading and versioning issues.
* **Compatibility:** Attributes now apply directly to items with zero configuration required, drastically improving compatibility with other external enchantment mods and datapacks.
* **Version Support:** Added official support for Minecraft 1.21.10.

---

### Version 0.3 (Major Update & Fixes)
* **Core Changes:** Fixed all baseline enchantment issues for **1.21.11+**.
* **New Systems:** Added an **Enchantment Upgrades system** and a Mute/Unmute toggle for the mod’s load message.
* **New Enchantments:**
* `Glass Breaker` (Requires Silk Touch)
* `Lightning Sword`
* `Anti-Gravity`
* `Self Repair`
* `Darkrai`, `Torrent`, and `Cresselia` enchants
* `Spawner Breaker` & `Mob Egg Grinder`
* `Inquisitive` *(Replaced Mob XP Bonus)*
* 11 unique `Mob Slayer` enchantments
* **Curses & Fun Modes:** Added `Curse of Sound` and `Baby Mode`.
* **Auras & Cosmetics:** Added **Invisible Item Frames** and new Aura Colors: *Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Purple, and RGB*.
* **Automation:** Added `Auto Farmland`, `Compact Totem`, `Super Firework`, and Auto-Planters for `Carrot`, `Potato`, `Wheat`, and `Beetroot`.
* **Balancing & Scaling:**
* Expanded levels for `Loyalty Speed` and `Advanced Riptide`.
* Added an extra level to `Equipment Drops Bonus`.
* Expanded `Mining Experience Bonus` and fixed its respective XP drop bugs.
* Updated `tooltip_order.json` to move the random book to the very bottom.
* **Fixes:** Fixed the head cosmetic issue.

* Fixed `Speed Boost III` and `Jump Boost III`.

* Fixed a tooltip misalignment caused by an enchantment name change.
